# InvoiAI Control Room

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Creative authority

The user asked for a vivid, polished and responsive redesign, gave permission to create the design direction, asked for Lottie motion, and requested a simpler dashboard with explanations moved to hover help. No existing brand kit, photography, footage, or exact visual references were supplied.

## Eight decisions

1. **Vibe**: vivid, assured, polished, selective, human.
2. **Journey**: arrival chaos, one controlled decision, operational clarity, selective access, focused invitation.
3. **Energy curve**: bright anticipation, quiet focus, a vivid control-room peak, then calm commitment.
4. **Feeling curve**: curious at entry, relieved when the workflow becomes legible, confident at the control-room peak, and included at the close. The remembered moment is an invoice travelling through the decision path while the dashboard reacts.
5. **Signature move**: the Control Pulse. Hovering or focusing a metric illuminates its linked queue and offers the explanation in place, never as permanent dashboard copy.
6. **Aesthetic range**: editorial product design with vivid cobalt and mint signals, not generic dark fintech.
7. **Structure**: distinct scenes, not a continuous worldflight.
8. **Assets**: no owned photography or footage supplied. Use code-native product surfaces and licensed LottieFiles animation as progressive enhancement.

## Tell-someone sentence

It is the site where a finance team can see the next safe decision before they open another spreadsheet.

## Journey and score

| Beat | Feeling | Device | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Anticipation | layered hero | Establish the product as a calm operating surface |
| Tension | Recognition | reveal | Name the costly handoff without a wall of copy |
| Turn | Relief | control pulse | Show one invoice becoming an informed decision |
| Substance | Confidence | horizontal range | Show focused AP capabilities with hover context |
| Selectivity | Desire | quiet spotlight | Present available launch bundles without invented scarcity claims |
| Commitment | Resolve | held close | One clear invitation |

## Scope refinement

The redesigned surface retains the former product map rather than reducing it: Platform, Workflows, Business, Industry, Resources, Pricing, FAQ, Docs, and Privacy remain reachable. The dashboard and authentication surfaces use the same visual family, with a light/dark control and contextual help that stays out of the way until it is needed.

## Peak

“I can see exactly what is safe to move and why.” The peak is the Control Pulse decision canvas.
